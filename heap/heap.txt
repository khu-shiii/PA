Heap is a complete binary tree thay follows a special property called the heap property.
1. Binary Tree - a tree in which every node can have atmost two children.
2. Complete BT - a binary tree in which:
                 every level is completely filled except possibly the last level.
                 the last level is filled from left to right.

Heaps follows two conditions:
1. it must be complete binary tree
2. it must follow the heap property

There are two types of heap:
1. Max Heap: in max heap, the parent node is always greater than or equal to its children.
2. Min Heap: in min heap, the parent node is always less than or equal to its children.

Difference between min heap and max heap
  __________________________________________________________
 |Feature                 Max heap             Min heap     |
  ----------------------------------------------------------
 |Root                     Max                   Min        |
 |Parent                  Greater               Smaller     |
 |Usage               Largest element        Smaller element|
 |Java        Priority Queue reverse order    Priority Queue|
 ------------------------------------------------------------
